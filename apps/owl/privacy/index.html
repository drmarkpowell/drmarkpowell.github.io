OWL Privacy Policy#

This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on OWL’s apps for iPhone, iPad, and any other devices and platforms.

Camera and Photo Library Access#

OWL requires camera and photo library access to detect and translate characters in images:

  • Camera Access: Used for real-time character detection. All processing happens on your device using CoreML. Camera frames are not saved, uploaded, or transmitted.
  • Photo Library Access: Used to analyze images you select. Photos are processed locally on your device and never leave your phone.
  • On-Device Processing: All image analysis is performed using Apple’s Vision and CoreML frameworks directly on your device. No images are sent to servers or third parties.

Machine Learning Models#

OWL uses a YOLO CoreML model for character detection:

  • The ML model is downloaded once during app installation
  • All inference (character detection) runs locally on your device
  • No detection data is collected, stored, or transmitted
  • Your images and detection results remain completely private

RevenueCat for Subscriptions#

OWL uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases:

  • An anonymous user ID is generated for purchase management
  • RevenueCat does not track any personal information about you
  • No information about what you detect or translate is shared with RevenueCat
  • RevenueCat only processes subscription status and purchase receipts

For more information about RevenueCat’s privacy practices, see their Privacy Policy.

Data Collection#

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage information.

OWL operates entirely on your device and does not collect information about you or your usage of the app. We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your activity or what you detect
  • Store your photos or detection results on our servers
  • Share your information with third parties
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Upload images to the cloud

Security#

All network data transfers in the OWL app (for subscription verification only) use HTTPS to keep your information secure.

Third-party links and content#

OWL is an independent tool for fictional alphabet detection and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any entertainment franchises.

California Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance#

We comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act. We therefore will not distribute your personal information to outside parties without your consent. (We collect no personal information anyway!)

Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance#

We never collect or maintain information at our website from those we actually know are under 13, and no part of our website is structured to attract anyone under 13. Since OWL does not collect any data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Information for European Union Customers#

OWL processes all data locally on your device. No personal information is transmitted to servers. Subscription data processed by RevenueCat may be stored outside of the EU, but this data is limited to anonymous purchase receipts and subscription status.

Your Rights#

Since OWL does not collect any personal data beyond anonymous subscription management, there is minimal data to access, modify, or delete. You maintain complete privacy while using the app.

To manage your subscription data:

  • View and manage subscriptions through the iOS Settings app
  • Cancel subscriptions at any time through App Store settings
  • Contact us if you need assistance with subscription-related data

Your Consent#

By using our apps, you consent to our privacy policy.

Contacting Us#

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us through the App Store or via email. Please note that data management should be done within the iOS Settings app, not via email requests, for security reasons.
